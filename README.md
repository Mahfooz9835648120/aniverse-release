## ✨ Features

### 🎌 Multi-Audio Playback
Watch every episode in your preferred language — **Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam, Telugu, Bengali, English, Japanese** — switched instantly without reloading.

### 📺 Massive Catalog
- **1150+ episodes** of One Piece (multi-audio through season 16)
- **500 episodes** of Naruto Shippuden with full multi-audio
- Dragon Ball Z, Hunter x Hunter, Fairy Tail, and 380+ more titles

### 🗂️ Smart Episode Navigation
- **Arc pills** for shows ≤100 eps per season (like Naruto's Kazekage Rescue, Long-Awaited Reunion arcs)
- **Range dropdown** for 100+ episode seasons (One Piece, Naruto)
- **TMDB / AniList toggle** — switch episode source for better thumbnails & titles
- 🟡 **Filler markers** on every filler episode

### ⏭️ Auto Skip
- Automatically skips **intros and outros**
- **Auto Next** — plays next episode when current one finishes

### 🖥️ Player
- **Server 1 & Server 2** with one-tap switching
- **Multi-audio player** with language selector (Hindi, Tamil, English, Japanese, Malayalam)
- Desktop: **prev/next episode arrows** flanking the player
- Smooth player controls with fade animation

### 🔍 Metadata
- **AniList + TMDB** integration for episode stills, titles, descriptions
- Airing countdown for ongoing shows
- Share & report buttons per episode
